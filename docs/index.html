How to read this

Cost of waiting is the second axis. Acuity asks how sick you are; this asks what waiting costs you, and it rises every minute. It is a declared scheduling policy, not an estimated causal quantity.

Because the curve is convex, a low-acuity patient eventually overtakes a higher one - the queue cannot starve anyone. Level 1 is the deliberate exception and is never overtaken.

Toggle to Status quo to see the same patients at the same minute ordered by acuity then arrival, with nobody re-ranked after triage.

The ▲n badge shows how many places a patient moves because of the policy you have selected, compared with the other ordering.

Watch P17 from minute 40. Every reading stays inside its normal range; the trajectory does not. She arrives level 3 and reaches level 1 by minute 95.
